Chief Technology Officer | Managing Partner

Monica Lillo

Monica holds a Bachelor of Computer and Information Sciences and assisted Calix to develop Dream Property’s real estate management app.

Her degree grounded her in software development, programming languages, databases, and systems design. Building the Dream Property platform expanded that into practical product work: web development for the company site and client-facing tools, and mobile development for iOS and Android so owners and tenants can manage their properties from any device.

Monica contributes across the stack: writing and refining application code, improving interfaces, and helping shape features that make everyday property management clearer and more efficient. Her focus is reliable software that supports the standard of service Dream Property is built on.
