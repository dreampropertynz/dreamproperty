Chief Executive Officer | Co-Founder

Calix Mojica

Calix brings residential property management experience and a Bachelor of Property from the University of Auckland to Dream Property. His degree gave him a strong understanding of property, investment, development, marketing, and the wider property industry. He has taken what he learned through his studies and combined it with his hands-on experience to look at real estate management from a different perspective.

This approach led Calix to develop Dream Property’s own real estate management app, creating smarter ways to manage everyday tasks, improve efficiency, and make real estate management easier for everyone involved. His goal is to keep finding new ways to use technology and fresh ideas to raise the standard of real estate services.
