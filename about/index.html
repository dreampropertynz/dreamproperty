About Dream Property

Dream Property is a New Zealand property management company and property marketplace, built on our own software. We bring the two together so that managing property and finding property feel simple, clear, and modern.

How we started

Dream Property began with a simple observation. Property management often depends on slow, disconnected tools and repeated manual work, and that costs owners, tenants, and managers valuable time.

After working in the property industry and seeing these problems up close, our founder set out to create a better way. Months of work went into building our own property management software, and Dream Property grew from that effort.

Property management

We provide residential and commercial property management for owners in Auckland. We look after the day-to-day work of a tenancy, including rent, inspections, maintenance, tenancy law requirements, and communication with everyone involved.

Our aim is simple. Owners get peace of mind knowing their investment is in good hands, and tenants get a home or workplace that is properly looked after.

The marketplace

The Dream Property marketplace is a place to search for property across New Zealand, including residential, commercial, and rural property. Owners advertise their own properties directly on the platform at a low cost, and people looking for a property can browse everything in one clear place.

Our technology

Everything we do is supported by software built by our own team. Our property management software brings tenancies, inspections, maintenance, communication, and records together in one place, so nothing gets lost between different tools.

The software is used exclusively by Dream Property today. That means every feature is tested on our own portfolio and improved using real, day-to-day experience.

What we believe

Property should be simple and stress-free. For us, that means clear communication, honest dealing, and technology that saves time instead of adding extra steps.

We want every owner, tenant, and visitor to feel looked after, and we want to earn their trust over the long term.

Based in Auckland

Dream Property Limited is based in Auckland, New Zealand. Our property management services focus on Auckland, and our marketplace is open to the whole country.

Have a question? Email us at info@dreamproperty.co.nz.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Depending on how you use our website and services, we may collect:

  • Listing information, such as the property address, photos, description, price, availability, and the contact details you choose to publish.
  • Property management information, such as owner, tenant, and applicant contact details, tenancy or lease details, rent and bond records, inspection reports, maintenance requests, and related communications. Tenant applications may include employment, income, and reference details that you choose to give us.
  • Messages and enquiries you send through the website or to info@dreamproperty.co.nz, and messages between users that pass through our platform.
  • Billing and transaction records. Payment card details are handled by our payment providers and are not stored by us.
  • Technical information, such as your IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.

How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you, from your use of the website, and from other users where they provide it as part of a listing, enquiry, or tenancy. With your authorisation, we may also collect it from referees, previous landlords, and identity or reference checking providers. We collect information only by lawful and fair means and only where it is reasonably necessary for our functions.

We use personal information to run the marketplace and your account, publish and manage listings, pass on enquiries, deliver property management services (such as leasing, rent collection, inspections, and maintenance), verify identities, prevent fraud and misuse, process payments, send service messages, improve our website and software, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. Where practical, we use de-identified or aggregated information to improve our software. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where needed for the purposes above. This includes other users (for example, an owner receiving a tenant application, or a contractor receiving access details for a repair), service providers who host our systems or provide email, analytics, payment, verification, and software services, our accountants, insurers, and professional advisers, and public bodies such as Tenancy Services, the Tenancy Tribunal, Inland Revenue, the courts, and the Police where required or permitted by law. Information you publish in a listing is public.

Some of our service providers store or process information outside New Zealand, including in Australia, the United States, and Europe. Before sending personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it will be protected by safeguards comparable to those in the Privacy Act 2020, unless you have authorised the disclosure after being told that such protection may not apply.

Cookies, analytics, and browser storage

We use website analytics to understand how our website performs, and we use your browser’s local storage to remember your language choice. We do not use this information to show third-party advertising. If we add other cookies, analytics, or advertising tools, we will update this policy. You can clear your browser storage or block cookies in your browser settings, although some features may not work as intended.

Marketing messages

We send marketing messages only with your consent or where the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 otherwise permits. Every marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe. Service messages, such as account notices and tenancy communications, are not marketing and may still be sent.

Your password is never stored

We do not store your password in a readable form. Dream Property will never ask you for your password by email, phone, text message, or social media. We use reasonable security safeguards to protect personal information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access, but no system is completely secure. If you think your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires, for example seven years for tax and financial records. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

Access and correction

You may ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email us and we will respond within 20 working days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and where the Privacy Act 2020 allows us to refuse or withhold information, we will explain why. You may withdraw your consent to marketing at any time. Privacy breaches

If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as the Privacy Act 2020 requires.

Children

Our website and services are for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Our website may link to other websites and services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its last updated date, is always the one shown on this website. If we make a significant change, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.

Contact and complaints

For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or complaints, contact our privacy officer at info@dreamproperty.co.nz. If we have not resolved your concern, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz or on 0800 803 909.
