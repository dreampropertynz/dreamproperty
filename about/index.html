About Dream Property

Dream Property is a New Zealand property marketplace and technology platform. We help people search, discover, buy, rent, and sell property across the country, including residential, commercial, and rural property.

The platform is designed for everyone in the property journey: buyers, sellers, renters, landlords, homeowners, tenants, agents, agencies, developers, and other legitimate participants.

Alongside the marketplace, Dream Property developed the dream property management tool. That software is used exclusively by Dream Property today and will be made available more widely once it is perfected.

Your dream. Your property. Your future.
