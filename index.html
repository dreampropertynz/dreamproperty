About Dream Property

Beyond expectations

At Dream Property, our goal is to provide real estate services that sets higher standards through genuine care, clear communication, professionalism, and innovation.

Calix recognised early in his real estate career that there was an opportunity to do things differently. Real estate management comes with challenges, from balancing the needs of owners and tenants to managing the everyday demands of the industry. He believed there had to be a smarter, more efficient way to deliver a better experience for everyone.

Driven by this vision, Calix spent several months developing an exclusive real estate management app designed to simplify administrative tasks, improve everyday operations, and work more efficiently.

That idea became the foundation of Dream Property.

We want to set the dream standard for real estate services. We use technology and innovation to make residential, commercial, and industrial real estate management easier, more efficient, and more professional. Our goal is to create a better experience for owners, tenants, and real estate professionals.

Following a successful start and positive client feedback, we are preparing for future growth beyond residential, commercial, and industrial real estate management. We are focused on strengthening our foundations, refining our systems, and continuing to deliver exceptional service.

Your Dream. Your Property. Your Future.

Director | Co-Founder

Calix Mojica

Calix brings residential property management experience and a Bachelor of Property from the University of Auckland to Dream Property. His degree gave him a strong understanding of property, investment, development, marketing, and the wider property industry. He has taken what he learned through his studies and combined it with his hands on experience to look at real estate management from a different perspective.

This approach led Calix to develop Dream Property’s own real estate management app, creating smarter ways to manage everyday tasks, improve efficiency, and make real estate management easier for everyone involved. His goal is to keep finding new ways to use technology and fresh ideas to raise the standard of real estate services.

Co-Founder

Rae Lillo

Rae is the Co-Founder of Dream Property. She holds a Bachelor of Psychology and is completing a Bachelor of Health Science (Nursing), giving her a strong foundation in communication, decision-making, and understanding individual needs.

Rae applies this knowledge to help shape a more thoughtful and people-focused approach to real estate management. Her background supports clear communication, empathy, and an understanding of how people respond to different situations, complementing Calix’s focus on real estate innovation and technology.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Dream Property collects, uses, protects, and shares personal information through this website and in connection with our real estate management services. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 and the information privacy principles it sets out.

Who we are

Dream Property provides residential, commercial, and industrial real estate management services in Auckland, New Zealand. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean Dream Property.

Your password is never stored

We do not store your password anywhere in a readable form. We never see, keep, or share your password in plain text, and we never record it in emails, documents, spreadsheets, or notes.

Dream Property will never ask you for your password by email, phone, text message, or social media. If someone contacts you claiming to be from Dream Property and asks for your password, do not share it and let us know straight away at info@dreamproperty.co.nz.

Information we may collect

We may collect information you provide when you contact us, enquire about a property, request a service, apply for a tenancy or lease, enter into a real estate management agreement, or otherwise communicate with us. This may include your name, contact details, property details, tenancy or lease details, and the content of your messages.

We only collect personal information that is necessary for a lawful purpose connected with our services, and we collect it directly from you wherever we reasonably can.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide real estate management services, communicate with owners and tenants, manage properties and tenancies or leases, arrange maintenance, process applications, meet our legal obligations, and carry out business administration. We will not use your information for a purpose unrelated to those above without your consent, unless the law allows or requires it.

Identity checks and legal requirements

In some circumstances the law may require us to verify identity or keep certain records, including under New Zealand anti-money laundering legislation, residential tenancy legislation, and tax requirements. Where this applies, we will let you know what we need and why.

Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with relevant service providers where necessary to provide real estate management, maintenance, tenancy, legal, accounting, insurance, or other related services. We only share what is needed, and we expect those providers to protect it. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, including to courts, the Tenancy Tribunal, the Disputes Tribunal, regulators, or the Police.

Information held or processed overseas

Some of the tools and services we use, including website hosting, may store or process information outside New Zealand. Where personal information is sent overseas, we take reasonable steps to make sure it is protected by comparable safeguards.

Website hosting, cookies, and third party content

This website is hosted by a third party and may include locally hosted video. Hosting providers may collect technical information such as your IP address, device type, and browser details, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Information security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. If a privacy breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and the people affected as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy or as the law requires, and then we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

Access and correction

You have the right to ask for access to the personal information we hold about you and to ask us to correct it. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframes required by the Privacy Act 2020.

Marketing messages

We will only send you marketing or promotional messages with your consent, and every such message will include a simple way to opt out, in line with the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007. Messages about your tenancy, lease, or property are service messages and are not marketing.

Privacy complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can put it right. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this website, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.

Contact

For privacy related enquiries, or to make an access or correction request, contact our privacy officer at info@dreamproperty.co.nz .
