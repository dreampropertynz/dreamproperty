Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Dream Property Limited collects, uses, protects, and shares personal information through this website, our marketplace, and related services. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020. The version shown on this website is the current version.

Who we are

Dream Property Limited operates a New Zealand property marketplace and technology platform. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean Dream Property Limited.

Your password is never stored in plain text

We do not store your password in a readable form. Dream Property will never ask you for your password by email, phone, text message, or social media. If someone claims to be from Dream Property and asks for your password, do not share it.

Information we may collect

We may collect information you provide when you contact us, create or attempt to create an account, start a listing, search addresses, use marketplace features, or otherwise communicate with us. This may include your name, contact details, property address details, and the content of your messages.

We only collect personal information that is necessary for a lawful purpose connected with our services, and we collect it directly from you wherever we reasonably can.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, operate and improve the website and marketplace, support listings and accounts, meet legal obligations, and carry out business administration. We will not use your information for an unrelated purpose without your consent, unless the law allows or requires it.

Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the platform, and where the law requires disclosure. We only share what is needed.

Information held or processed overseas

Some tools we use, including website hosting, may store or process information outside New Zealand. Where personal information is sent overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure comparable safeguards.

Website, cookies, and third party services

This website may use hosting providers and similar technologies that collect technical information such as IP address, device type, and browser details. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Address and property information

Address lookup may use New Zealand Post address data under applicable terms. Property attribute data, where shown, is drawn from authorised or licensed sources and may be incomplete or not current.

Information security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and affected people as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

Access and correction

You may ask for access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframes required by the Privacy Act 2020.

Privacy complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, contact us first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Contact

For privacy related enquiries, contact our privacy officer via DREAM·E on this website, or by email when account services are available.

Marketplace

Listing Rules

These rules help keep Dream Property fair, accurate, and safe for everyone.

Who may list

You may list a property only if you are the owner or have clear authority to list it. Agents and agencies must be appropriately verified before listing under an agency identity.

Accuracy

Listings must be truthful. Do not use misleading photos, incorrect prices, or false availability. Describe the property honestly.

Photos

Use clear, recent photos of the actual property. Do not use stock images as if they were the property, and avoid heavy editing that misrepresents the home.

Prohibited content

Do not post spam, scams, offensive material, or listings for properties you cannot lawfully offer.

Moderation

We may remove or limit listings that breach these rules. A report does not automatically mean a user is at fault. Reports are reviewed fairly.

Safety

Safety & Reporting

Stay cautious when dealing with property online. Dream Property will never ask for your password by email, phone, text, or social media.

Viewings

If a property is occupied, be respectful of the people living there and of the lister's time. Preferred viewing times may not always be available.

Reporting

You can report listings for spam, fraud, fake property, incorrect information, misleading photos, or other policy issues from the property detail page. Reports are reviewed by our team.

Scams

Do not send money or personal documents to anyone based only on a listing message. If something feels wrong, stop and report it.
