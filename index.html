About Dream Property

A different dream for property

Dream Property began with a simple idea. Property is about more than buildings. It is about what people want to achieve.

Prashant Jeet founded Auckland Property Rentals & Management with a focus on giving landlords and tenants a service built on trust, care, and clear communication.

As the business grew, so did the vision. Property is not just about renting or managing a home. It can be about buying a first home, building an investment portfolio, selling a property, or creating something for the future.

Calix reached out to Prashant after seeing the potential in what he had built. Together, they saw an opportunity to create a brand that could represent this bigger vision.

That is where Dream Property was born.

The name represents what property means to everyone. A dream home, a dream investment, a dream portfolio, or simply a better future.

Dream Property is here to support people throughout their property journey, from renting and managing to buying, selling, investing, and beyond.

Your Dream. Your Property. Your Future.
